# Cost Explorer

Administration › AWS › **Cost Explorer** shows the costs of this AWS account from AWS Cost Explorer: the **Total** (**This month**, **Month-end forecast**, **Last month**), the costs **By service**, the costs **Daily (last 30 days)**, and the costs **By cost allocation tag** when a tag is set.

The costs are fetched once a day by a scheduled task, not when the page is opened; the page shows when they were fetched.

More: [Redmine AWS Cost Explorer](https://redmine-kit.github.io/redmine_aws_costexplorer/).
