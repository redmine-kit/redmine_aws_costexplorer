# Cost Explorer

Administration › Serverless › **Cost Explorer** shows the **Costs of this AWS account** from AWS Cost Explorer: **This month so far**, the **Forecast for this month**, the **Previous month**, the costs **By service**, the costs **Daily, last 30 days**, and the costs by a cost allocation tag when one is set.

The costs are fetched once a day by a scheduled task, not when the page is opened; the page shows when they were fetched.

More: [Redmine Serverless Cost Explorer](https://redmine-kit.github.io/redmine_serverless_costexplorer/).
